Chicken Biryani is my number one favorite because I like its combination of rice, chicken, spices, and aroma.

3.🍗 Chicken Biryani Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Chicken
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Green Chilli
  • Mint Leaves
  • Coriander
  • Curd
  • Biryani Masala
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt

4.👨‍🍳 How I Make Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the rice.
  2. Marinate the chicken with spices and curd.
  3. Fry onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook the marinated chicken.
  5. Add the soaked rice.
  6. Add water and spices.
  7. Cook the rice and chicken together.
  8. Add mint and coriander.
  9. Cook until the rice is completely done.
  10. 1. Serve hot.
